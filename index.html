Taehwa Yoo

PhD Student, Department of Astronomy, University of Florida

I am PhD student at the department of Astronomy in the University of Florida. I am interested in star formation in general. My biggest questions are how stellar mass is determined and how massive stars form. I'm trying to answer these questions by observing high-mass star-forming region with instruments like ALMA, VLA and JWST.

Research Overview

During my PhD, I'm working on the W51 star-forming region, which is one of the most massive star-forming regions in our Galaxy. I led the JWST NIRCam/MIRI imaging observations of W51 (GO 6151) and analyzed the data to study the star formation in this region.
