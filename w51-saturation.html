W51 JWST saturation recovery

The brightest parts of the star-forming region W51 saturated the JWST NIRCam and MIRI detectors (program GO 6151): those pixels collected more light than the detector can record, and the pipeline left many of them without a value. This project restores them, using what each detector kept: the reads before saturation, the first read, the detector's response curve, the shape of stars, and the other filters. The notebooks below record the progress, newest stage last. Each opens as a page with its figures.
